# Human AI Companion

MANIFEX subsystem implementing a human-centered lifelong development and opportunity model.

## Worlds

- Personal: private Life Log, memory, reflection, emotional support.
- Development: learning, tutoring, assessment, practice, projects, discovery, verification.
- Professional: consented capability evidence, credentials, portfolio, profile, opportunity matching.

## Authority

Human authority is sovereign. This subsystem cannot diagnose, manipulate, control life decisions, replace human relationships, determine human worth, or make consequential decisions on a person's behalf.

## Evidence

Capability claims require an evidence trail and provenance. Credentials require verified evidence and human approval.

## Integration

This subsystem is designed to bind to MANIFEX's existing evidence, provenance, verification, evaluation, capability registry, TwinLab, gate, human-authority, and engineering-graph fabrics rather than create parallel governance.

## Status

Core contracts and boundary implementation are present on `feature/human-ai-companion`. Full MANIFEX runtime integration, external credential interoperability, production storage, identity, authentication, and end-to-end deployment remain qualification/integration work and are intentionally not represented as complete by this package.
