# MANIFEX Human AI Companion — Subsystem Specification v1

Status: IMPLEMENTATION BASELINE / QUALIFICATION REQUIRED

## 1. Mission

Every human deserves the opportunity to learn, explore, fail, create, discover strengths they did not know they possessed, develop capabilities, demonstrate what they can do, and pursue opportunities based on what they can actually demonstrate.

## 2. Constitutional invariant

The Companion may help a human discover and demonstrate capability, but it never becomes the authority over the human.

## 3. Three worlds

### Personal World
Private by default: life experiences, emotions, relationships, reflections, private goals, conversations, and Life Log. No automatic professional disclosure.

### Development World
Controlled learning activity: assessments, curriculum, tutoring, practice, simulations, projects, challenges, feedback, capability assessments, and credentials. The human performs the work; the Companion facilitates and evaluates the learning process.

### Professional World
Consent-based sharing: resume, cover letter, verified skills, projects, credentials, selected assessments, capability evidence, professional progress, and portfolio.

Cross-world transfer requires an explicit, purpose-bound consent grant. Consent is revocable and may expire.

## 4. Core modules

Understand; Learn; Discover; Grow; Build; Create; Experience; Verify; Connect; Learning Engine; Discovery Engine; Life Log; Capability System; Credential System; Human Capability Passport; Professional Profile; Opportunity Matching; Employer Evidence Sharing.

## 5. Evidence authenticity

Meaningful capability claims follow:

CLAIM → ASSESSMENT → RAW RESULT → ARTIFACT/DEMONSTRATION → VERIFICATION → PROVENANCE → CREDENTIAL → PORTABLE CAPABILITY RECORD

A credential is a conclusion backed by an evidence package. A credential is not itself the evidence.

## 6. Evidence states

REPORTED, OBSERVED, ASSESSED, DEMONSTRATED, VERIFIED, INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED, HUMAN_CONFIRMED, PROFESSIONAL_FINDING.

AI observations and hypotheses must not be represented as professional findings.

## 7. Capability model

Capabilities are defined against explicit requirements and evidence. Development state includes demonstrated strengths, gaps, evidence confidence, progression, and a "What I Don't Know" representation. Capability scores describe a defined capability or assessment; they do not measure human worth or general intelligence.

## 8. Discovery

Discovery combines interest, experiment, performance, persistence, learning, user feedback, and real-world experience to generate revisable strength hypotheses and career possibilities. The Companion proposes exploration; the human chooses.

## 9. Learning

The Learning Engine assesses current capability, compiles an appropriate curriculum, teaches, tutors, generates practice, supports projects, analyzes failure, adapts difficulty, retests, and verifies demonstrated capability. It must not force already-demonstrated prerequisites.

## 10. Life Log

The Life Log records human-reported experiences and Companion-organized observations. It may support reflection and pattern exploration. It is not automatically professional evidence and must not be silently converted into employment data.

## 11. Medical boundary

The Companion may educate about psychology, behavior, and health information; identify patterns or hypotheses; organize observations; and suggest professional evaluation when appropriate. It does not medically diagnose. Qualified human professionals determine medical diagnoses.

## 12. Human Capability Passport

The Passport is person-owned and designed for portable selective disclosure. It contains credentials, verified capabilities, assessments, demonstrations, projects, certifications, work experience, portfolio references, evidence references, and provenance. MANIFEX must not require continued platform membership to retain or present a person's legitimate exported records.

## 13. Credentials

MANIFEX competency credentials represent defined MANIFEX competency requirements that have been met through evidence and required review. They must not be represented as accredited academic degrees or legal licenses unless separately awarded by the appropriate authority.

## 14. Professional opportunity

Employers define capability requirements. The system can match permissioned candidate evidence to those requirements and present supporting evidence. The system does not determine candidate worth or make the hiring decision. The human employer decides.

## 15. Security and privacy

Privacy by default; explicit consent; purpose limitation; least privilege; subject isolation; revocation; expiration; auditability; provenance; data minimization; export/delete; anti-gaming controls; and separation of personal, development, and professional domains.

## 16. Human authority gates

Personal consequential decisions remain with the person. Medical decisions requiring diagnosis remain with qualified human professionals. Employment decisions remain with human employers. Credential approval follows defined human governance. Consequential system actions require explicit human authorization.

## 17. MANIFEX integration

The Companion consumes MANIFEX's existing Evidence Fabric, Provenance, Verification Engine, Evaluation Fabric, Capability Registry, TwinLab, Gate Engine, Human Authority, and Engineering Graph. It does not create a parallel trust/governance system.

## 18. Verification and anti-gaming

Tests must cover assessment integrity, artifact provenance, identity binding, replay, independent verification, adversarial evaluation, evidence freshness, credential integrity, privacy isolation, consent enforcement, and unauthorized cross-world disclosure.

## 19. Lifecycle

SPECIFY → GRAPH → CONTRACT → IMPLEMENT → TEST → TWINLAB ATTACK → VERIFY → HUMAN REVIEW → APPROVE → INTEGRATE.

Implementation status must never be inferred from specification completeness.

## 20. Non-goals

The Companion is not a controller of the person's life, a replacement for human relationships, a medical diagnostician, an authority over human worth, an autonomous hiring authority, or a mechanism for emotional dependency.
